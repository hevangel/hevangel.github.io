What is preserved
The page loads the original MVL7 packages, two a2901 architectures, component labs, documentation, and translated test vectors without changing them. The vectors still drive a 10 ns clock and their original assertions provide the pass/fail oracle.
Compatibility boundary
This is a source-level behavioral compatibility engine derived from alg_beh2901.vhdl, not a general VHDL compiler or cycle-accurate GHDL build. The experimental GHDL browser ports do not yet provide complete scheduling and signal decoding for this legacy multi-file MVL7 design.
Static by design
Parsing, simulation, waveform rendering, assertion checks, and VCD export happen locally. No source or simulation data leaves the browser, and GitHub Pages needs no compile service.
