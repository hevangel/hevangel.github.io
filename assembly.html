What survived
Eleven original .ASM files and their 2500 A.D. assembler .LST outputs, dated June 24–25, 1997. The integrated project is a signed four-function calculator with serial output and a scanned 4×4 keypad.
Why a source model?
The object files, final linked image, board ROM, and linker map did not survive. The workbench therefore follows the preserved source control flow and models its registers, stack, ACIA, keypad, and calculator state; it does not claim binary- or cycle-accurate emulation.
Debugger tips
Click a highlighted executable line to set a breakpoint. Run continues to input or a breakpoint; Step executes one modeled instruction. Press 1 at the menu, then try 12+3Enter.
